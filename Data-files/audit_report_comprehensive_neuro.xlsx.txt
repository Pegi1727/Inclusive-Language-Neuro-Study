Pattern of differences: Intercept N400 is off by exactly +2.1155; i.e. reported

**Tolerance ±0.05 exceeded for essentially every coefficient.**

Item random-intercept variances are order-of-magnitude larger than the observed
between-item variance → implausible / not reproducible from these data.

  RMS/PTP columns before submission (Case C for those parts).
